## Description (4000 car.)
```
Bedtime Routine turns the evening wind-down into a calm, predictable ritual your little one can actually understand.

A big friendly hourglass shows the time passing — sand flowing, then flipping — so toddlers can SEE how long is left in each step, without numbers or nagging. The evening moves gently from one step to the next, and always ends with the goodnight moon.

WHY PARENTS LOVE IT
• Visual time, not abstract minutes — perfect for ages 1 to 4
• Predictable steps reduce bedtime battles
• Calm, dark design made for the moment before sleep
• A short opening animation previews tonight's steps, so there are no surprises

BUILD YOUR OWN ROUTINE
Start from the classic steps — pajamas, story, bottle, teeth, cuddle — or pick from a library of ready-made steps grouped in three families:
• Care & hygiene: potty, diaper, bath, face wash, lotion, medicine
• Getting ready for night: tidy up, prepare the room, night light, teddy & pacifier, glass of water, feed the pet
• Quiet moments: nursing, lullaby, talk about the day, calm breathing, prayer, kisses for everyone
Set the duration and order of every step. It's your routine.

MADE FOR EVERY FAMILY
• Free, forever — no ads
• No account, no sign-up
• Nothing that identifies your child
• Works fully offline
• 27 languages, with automatic detection

Bedtime Routine is a labour of love, built by a dad for his own toddler. If it brings a little calm to your evenings, you can support it from the app — but it will always stay free.

Sweet dreams.
```
